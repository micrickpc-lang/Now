## Dependency Gate

`npm audit --omit=dev --audit-level=high` passed after the Prisma, Nodemailer,
Google Auth, Next/PostCSS and Swagger/js-yaml updates. CI generates and uploads
a CycloneDX SBOM for the same production dependency graph. Development-only tool
advisories are not treated as deployable runtime findings; they remain covered by
the lockfile, CI tests and filesystem/container scans.
